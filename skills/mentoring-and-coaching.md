# Mentoring and coaching

> Growing other people's judgment and skill on purpose, one conversation and one review at a time.

## In practice

Give people problems slightly beyond them and stay close enough that failing is safe. Ask before
telling, and tell when asking would waste their week. Review work for the thinking behind it,
not only the result, and say what was good as precisely as what was not. Build the places —
communities of practice, pairing, retrospectives — where people teach each other without you.
