# Organization design

> Deciding how a growing company divides its work, its decisions and its people.

## In practice

Draw the teams around the products or domains they own, not around the technologies they
use. Decide what a team may decide alone and what needs the room. Design for the next size,
not the current one — including where a distributed or near-shore team fits — and recruit
and develop the leaders each new shape requires.
