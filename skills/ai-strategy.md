# AI strategy

> Deciding where and how a company adopts AI, and the sequence, investment and capabilities that get it there.

## In practice

Assess which business activities AI can change materially and which it cannot yet. Set the
direction for adoption across plan, change and run, with the platforms, data and skills it
requires. Choose between building, buying and partnering per use case, and revisit the choices
as the models and the market move.
