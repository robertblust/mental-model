# Stakeholder and vendor management

> Keeping the people who fund, use and supply a platform aligned.

## In practice

At 3AP, advised clients including Credit Suisse, Swisscard, AXA and Mobiliar, and led
solution-architecture bids across public and private sector work, including public tenders
under Swiss procurement law and WTO rules. This work meant aligning what a client's
stakeholders needed with what a vendor or delivery team could commit to.
