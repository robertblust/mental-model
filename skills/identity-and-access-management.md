# Identity and access management

> Controlling who can authenticate to systems and what they are authorized to do once inside.

## In practice

Design and configure authentication and authorization mechanisms, including protocols such as
OAuth or OIDC, that verify identity and grant access. Define roles, permissions and access
policies and review them to keep access aligned with actual need. Investigate access-related
incidents and remove or adjust access as roles change. Typical tools: OAuth, OIDC, Okta.
