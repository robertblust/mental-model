# Program management

> Coordinating a portfolio of related projects so that, together, they deliver a larger set of business benefits than any one project alone.

## In practice

Define the structure and governance across a set of interdependent projects, aligning scope,
sequencing and shared resources. Track cross-project risks and dependencies and manage the
overall business case and benefits realization. Report progress to sponsors and adjust the
program as priorities or constraints shift.
