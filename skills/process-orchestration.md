# Process orchestration

> Coordinating the execution of multi-step business processes across systems and services using a dedicated orchestration engine.

## In practice

Model processes as executable workflows and configure an engine to run, track and recover them
across their lifecycle. Integrate process steps with the underlying systems and services that
perform the actual work. Monitor running process instances and handle failures or exceptions so
processes complete correctly. Typical tools: Camunda, Zeebe, AWS Step Functions.
