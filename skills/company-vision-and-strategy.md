# Company vision and strategy

> Where a company is going and why, and the few bets that get it there.

## In practice

Say what the company is for in a sentence people repeat without you. Choose the markets,
products and capabilities it will commit to, and name the ones it will not. Turn the choice
into a plan with owners, money and dates, and read the market back into it every quarter.
Carry the vision into hiring, funding and partnership conversations until it is the company's,
not the founders'.
