# Engineering leadership

> Leading and growing software engineering organizations, balancing technical direction with people and delivery outcomes.

## In practice

Set technical direction and standards across one or more engineering teams and grow the
organization's structure to match. Hire, coach and develop engineers and managers, and build
the processes that let teams deliver reliably at scale. Balance long-term technical investment
against short-term delivery pressure and represent engineering priorities to the rest of the
organization.
