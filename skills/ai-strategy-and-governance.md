# AI strategy and governance

> Deciding how a company adopts AI, under what oversight and with which guardrails.

## In practice

Developed and executed LIKE MAGIC's company-wide AI strategy, rolling out AI assistants
across plan, change and run under a Human Oversight principle. Set data-privacy guardrails
so internal context is never used to train external LLMs, alongside cost governance for
multi-provider AI tooling.
