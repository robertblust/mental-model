# Process orchestration and automation

> Making a business process explicit and executable.

## In practice

Drove process orchestration with Camunda for LIKE MAGIC's end-to-end guest journey, from
booking through check-in, stay and check-out — a reference case with a 95% self-check-in
rate, 9.0/10 guest happiness and 7,000 process instances per month. Also uses n8n for
lighter-weight automation, and works with BPMN 2.0 and DMN as the underlying notations for
making a process explicit and executable.
