# Product discovery

> Finding out whether a product should exist before building it — the problem, the buyer, the price, the no.

## In practice

Write the idea down as a claim that can be wrong: who has the problem, what they pay for
today, what would make them switch. Put the smallest real thing in front of them — a talk, a
model, a worked example — and collect the objections rather than the compliments. Set the
outcome you will accept before you start, including a clean no, and publish what you
learned either way.
