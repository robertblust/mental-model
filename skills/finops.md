# FinOps

> Managing cloud and technology spend so cost stays visible, accountable and aligned with the value delivered.

## In practice

Track cloud and technology spend against budgets and attribute it to the teams or products
responsible. Identify opportunities to reduce waste, such as underused resources or inefficient
pricing commitments, and work with engineering teams to act on them. Build reporting and
forecasts that let the organization make informed trade-offs between cost and performance.
Typical tools: AWS Cost Explorer, CloudHealth, Kubecost.
