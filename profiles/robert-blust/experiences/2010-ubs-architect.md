# IT Architect — Software Development Lifecycle Toolchain

> Architected the integration and SDLC toolchains for UBS Wealth Management & Swiss Banking, and drove the division's business architecture and capability modeling on EMF/Ecore.

## Achievements

- Architected the SDLC toolchain for UBS Wealth Management & Swiss Banking — the environment several hundred engineers across the division planned, built and released in (Jira, Confluence, GitHub Enterprise, Maven, Jenkins, Sonar), including the offshore-capable setup for decentralized agile development and its security and network design for external partner access.
- Established API and data management for the division: a canonical data model, platform-independent definition of data types, interfaces and service contracts, generated XML-Schema and WSDL artifacts, a central API and interface-portfolio repository — and the role-based review process every team's interfaces had to pass. Built on Eclipse EMF, Xcore, Xtext and Xtend with a CDO model repository, drawing on outcomes from the Eclipse Modeling Platform Working Group.
- Drove the division's business architecture and capability modeling on EMF/Ecore, representing UBS internationally in the Eclipse Modeling Platform Working Group alongside Airbus, Ericsson, Alcatel-Lucent and SWIFT.
- Co-led the Java Chief Programmer community of the division for five years — the forum in which its lead engineers set common practice.
- Scrum Master and technical lead in central WM&SB initiatives.
- Spoke at UBS-internal brown-bag sessions on modeling, toolchain and architecture.

## Ending

Left in March 2015 after 16 years and four roles — engineering, methodology, then architecture.
Enough to back myself as a founder, and probably a little naive about what founding takes; I
wanted to find out. Co-founded 3AP the month after.
