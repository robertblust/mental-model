# Decide well over build fast

> AI moved the constraint from building quickly to deciding correctly; the decision is the work.

## In practice

Write the decision down before the code, with the alternatives that lost and why, so the
next person can disagree with a reason rather than a rewrite. Spend the saved build time on
the choice, not on building the wrong thing faster.

Not this: a feature that shipped in a day and is argued about for a quarter, because nobody
can say what it was for.
