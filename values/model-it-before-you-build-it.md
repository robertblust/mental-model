# Model it before you build it

> A business described as concepts and capabilities is one that people and AI can both act inside of.

## In practice

Keep a written model — domains, capabilities, processes, rules — that the code, the org chart
and the agents all refer back to, and keep it where the people who own the facts can edit it.
Build from the model, not beside it.

Not this: the model living in three heads and a slide deck, with every system encoding a
slightly different version of it.
