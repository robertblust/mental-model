# A continuing-education master's in AI leadership

> By the horizon I hold a MAS, a continuing-education master's, in AI leadership, earned beside the architect role and on my own account.

## What it makes true

The claim that AI is used with responsibility inside a company, which my profile makes, rests on a qualification and not only on work done: a CAS, a DAS and a MAS in how AI is led and governed in an organization, each an entry of the Education kind in the model with its diploma behind it. It holds when the MAS diploma exists; it fails if the path stops at the CAS or the DAS, which the education decision keeps as my call to make. It does not make me a data scientist, and it is not a consecutive MSc; what falls outside it is any qualification the role itself would have to grant.
