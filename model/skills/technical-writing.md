# Technical writing

> Writing that lets a reader understand and act on a technical thing without asking the author.

## In practice

Decide what the reader needs to do after reading, and cut everything that does not serve
it. State decisions, the alternatives that lost and the reasons, so the text survives its
author. Keep one term per concept and the same term throughout. Show the real example
rather than the generic one, and read the text back as the person who was not in the
room.
