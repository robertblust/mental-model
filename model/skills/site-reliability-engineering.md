# Site reliability engineering

> Applying engineering methods to operations so that systems meet defined reliability targets at scale.

## In practice

Define service level objectives and error budgets and use them to decide when to prioritize
reliability work over new features. Automate operational tasks that would otherwise require
repetitive manual effort and build tooling that makes systems easier to diagnose and recover.
Lead or contribute to incident response and follow up with changes that reduce the chance of
recurrence.
