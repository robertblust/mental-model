# UX design

> Designing how people use a product: what they see, in what order, and what they can do next.

## In practice

Work out who uses the product and what each of them came to do, then decide the structure that serves it: which pages or screens exist, what each one is for and what leads to what. Turn that into layouts, a type scale, color and components kept in one design system, so every page is built from the same parts and reads as one product. Prefer a design that holds for content not yet written over a screen drawn for each case. Review the result where it is used, rendered at the widths and themes people have, and hold it to legibility and accessibility as it grows. Typical tools: design tokens, component libraries, Figma, browser testing.
