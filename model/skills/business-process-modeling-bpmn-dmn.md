# Business process modeling (BPMN, DMN)

> Diagramming business processes and decision logic using standardized notations so they can be analyzed, communicated and automated.

## In practice

Map process flows and decision rules using notations such as BPMN for process steps and DMN for
decision logic. Work with process owners to validate that the model reflects how work actually
happens rather than how it is assumed to happen. Use the resulting models to identify
inefficiencies or to hand off to process automation tooling. Typical tools: BPMN, DMN, Camunda.
