# Public speaking

> Explaining technical work to a room so that people leave able to act on it.

## In practice

Pick the one idea the audience should take home and build the talk backward from it. Show the real system, the real numbers and the real mistake, not the slide version. Rehearse until the timing is yours, then leave room for the questions that tell you what landed.
