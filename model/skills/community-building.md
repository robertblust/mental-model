# Community building

> Making a place where practitioners teach each other, and keeping it alive after the founding energy is gone.

## In practice

Start with a recurring occasion — a meetup, a working group, a community of practice — and a
reason to come that is not you. Hand roles to members early so the group survives your
absence. Connect the community outward, to the projects, vendors and standards bodies it
depends on, and measure it by what members do for each other.
