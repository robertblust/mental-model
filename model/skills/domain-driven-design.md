# Domain-driven design

> Structuring software around the business domain's own concepts and language rather than around technical layers.

## In practice

Work with domain experts to build a shared vocabulary and model of the business domain. Identify bounded contexts and use them to draw system and team boundaries that mirror the domain's natural divisions. Keep the code's structure and terminology aligned with that domain model as understanding deepens.
