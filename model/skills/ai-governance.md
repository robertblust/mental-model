# AI governance

> The decision rights, policies and oversight under which a company uses AI.

## In practice

Define who may deploy which kind of AI system for which purpose, and under what human
oversight. Set the guardrails for data use, model providers and cost, and the review that
checks they hold. Keep the record regulators and customers will ask for, and adjust the rules
when the law or the technology changes.
