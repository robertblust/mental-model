# Conversational AI

> Systems that hold a dialogue with guests, customers or staff across several channels, increasingly with an AI agent answering rather than a person.

## In practice

Decide whether to build the conversational layer or integrate a specialist vendor, and design
the boundary either way: which channels are unified, what the AI may answer on its own, and
when a person takes over. Feed it the messaging history, identity and operational data it needs
to answer specifically rather than generically. Keep the handover visible in one inbox so no
conversation is dropped between the agent and the staff member. Watch what it does at the edge
of what it knows, and make it escalate rather than guess.
