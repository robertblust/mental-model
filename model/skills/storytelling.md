# Storytelling

> Ordering facts into an arc an audience will follow, and holding that arc across every piece that tells it.

## In practice

Decide the one claim the whole arc has to land, and cut what does not carry it. Order the
pieces so each stands alone and still moves the arc — the first earns attention, the last
asks for something. Fix what may be said before drafting starts, so the arc survives a
co-author or a generating tool. Keep the claim checkable, because an audience that can
verify one thing will grant the rest.
