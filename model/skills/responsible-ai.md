# Responsible AI

> Assessing and managing the ethical, fairness and safety implications of AI systems across their lifecycle.

## In practice

Assess AI systems for bias, fairness and potential harm before and after deployment. Define and
apply principles or policies that guide acceptable use and set boundaries on higher-risk
applications. Set up review and monitoring processes that surface ethical or safety concerns as
systems are used in practice.
