# Data protection (GDPR)

> Ensuring an organization's handling of personal data complies with data protection law and respects individuals' privacy rights.

## In practice

Assess how personal data is collected, used and stored and identify where processing needs a legal basis or additional safeguards. Run or support data protection impact assessments and maintain records of processing activities. Handle data subject requests and breach notifications and advise other parts of the organization on compliant data handling.
