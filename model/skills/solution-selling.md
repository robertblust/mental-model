# Solution selling

> Winning work by showing a client the solution to their problem before they have paid for it.

## In practice

Read the tender or the brief for the problem behind the requirements. Shape a solution architecture that answers it, price it honestly and present it as the people who will build it. Lead the bid across proposal, pitch and negotiation, and walk away from work that cannot be delivered as promised.
