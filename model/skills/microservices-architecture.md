# Microservices architecture

> Designing a system as a set of independently deployable services organized around business capabilities.

## In practice

Decompose a system into services with clear boundaries and define how those services communicate and share data. Design for independent deployment, resilience to partial failure and observability across service boundaries. Weigh the added operational complexity against the flexibility gained from decoupling services. Typical tools: Kubernetes, Docker, Istio.
