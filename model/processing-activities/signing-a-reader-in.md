# Signing a reader in

> Letting a reader the owner has invited sign in to the chat, so that it knows which models the reader may choose.

## Data subjects

- Readers the owner has invited to sign in to the chat

## Personal data

- The reader's name and email address, which their account at id.blust.ch holds
- The roles granted to the reader, which name the models they may choose
- For a reader who signs in with Google, the link to their Google account, its id and email address, which their account at id.blust.ch keeps until the link or the account is removed
- For a reader who signs in with Microsoft, the link to their Microsoft account, its id and the email address it names, which their account at id.blust.ch keeps until the link or the account is removed
- For a reader who signs in with Microsoft while that account is not yet linked, whether it is a personal account or which domains their organization has verified with Microsoft, an answer id.blust.ch's link check asks Microsoft for with that sign-in's access token, uses only to decide whether to link the account and keeps nowhere
- The tokens the sign-in issues, kept in the reader's browser, and the access token each message carries to the chat, which checks it

## Processors

| Processor | Receives |
| --- | --- |
| Google Cloud | The reader's name, email address and roles, and the link to their Google or Microsoft account where they made one, held by id.blust.ch in its Zurich region, and the access token each message carries; also what the link check reads of each sign-in with Google or Microsoft, in the same region and kept nowhere |
| Google | That the reader signs in to id.blust.ch, only when they choose to sign in with Google |
| Microsoft | That the reader signs in to id.blust.ch, only when they choose to sign in with Microsoft, and, while that account is not yet linked, the access token of that sign-in, with which the link check asks Microsoft about the account and its organization |
