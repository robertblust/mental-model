# Counting a reader's month

> Counting what a signed-in reader's answers cost in the month, so that no reader spends more of the chat's month than their own share.

## Data subjects

- Readers who sign in to the chat and send a message

## Personal data

- What the reader's answers cost this month, kept under a key made from their account that the chat cannot turn back into it

## Processors

| Processor | Receives |
| --- | --- |
| Google Cloud | What the reader's answers cost this month, in its Zurich region, under a key the chat cannot turn back into the account |
