# Narrate

> Turn each changed note into a clip in its language, and let none of them out unheard.

## What it takes

A deck whose English notes are reviewed and whose German notes were made from them and reviewed, both on a branch. The cues in a note are the author's directions for where to pause.

## Activities

1. The Narrator reads each slide's note from the deck, in each language; a cue becomes a paragraph break or a direction to the voice.
2. The Narrator skips a slide whose stamp still matches its note, voice, model and settings, so an edited note costs one clip and not a deck.
3. The Narrator speaks each remaining note in its language's voice and treats a click at the end of a clip.
4. The Owner listens to every new clip in the deck, in both languages.

## What it produces

| Deliverable | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Clip | One per changed slide and language, beside the deck, with the stamp that says what it was spoken from; finished when the Owner has heard it |

## What it never does

- Never lets one clip that fails stop the rest of the deck.
- Never regenerates a whole deck to change one clip.

## Gate

- The Owner has listened to every new clip.
- Every clip's stamp matches the note on its slide.

Where a clip cannot be made to sound right, the Owner decides whether the note changes, back through Delivery, or the voice does.
