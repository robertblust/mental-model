# Propose

> Put one decision to the Owner with a recommendation, and take the Owner's word on it.

## What it takes

An overview that has passed Survey. Where the overview is older than the question now asked, it goes back to Survey first.

## Activities

1. The Surveyor names the one decision that comes next and what it changes.
2. The Surveyor recommends one answer and says why, and names the other answers it set aside.
3. The Owner decides: yes, no, wait, or an answer of their own.

## What it produces

| Deliverable | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Proposal | One decision with one recommended answer and the reason for it |
| Owner's word | The answer the Owner gave, in their words; finished when it says what may be done |

## What it never does

- Never puts a second decision to the Owner before the first is answered.
- Never takes an earlier word of the Owner's as the answer to this decision.

## Gate

- The Owner has answered the decision put to them.

## If not met

| Outcome | Leads to |
| --- | --- |
| surveyed again | Survey |
| deferred | |

Deferring is a decision too.
