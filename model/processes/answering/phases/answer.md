# Answer

> Give the visitor what the model says, in their words, and not a claim more.

## What it takes

A message the fence has let through: under the length it allows, from a page the deployment names, inside the hour's, the day's and the month's share. The host's tools, at its pinned commit.

## Activities

1. The Visitor asks, in their own words and language.
2. The Answerer asks the host's tools, up to four rounds: a kind of thing by its type, a named thing by search and then the entity itself.
3. The Answerer writes the answer from what the tools said, naming the entity each claim rests on, and where they said nothing, that the model does not say.
4. The page links each entity the answer was read from, to the model page and to the file at the commit.
5. Nobody reads the answer before the Visitor does. The Owner's gate was passed once, before any question: the rules the seat runs under, the commit the host pins, the fence, and the switch that stops the chat without a deploy.

## What it produces

| Deliverable | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Answer | In the visitor's tab, in their language, every claim traceable to a tool's answer in that conversation; gone when the tab closes |

## What it never does

- Never sends a message before the visitor presses send, and never reads one they did not.
- Never answers a question that is not about the model with more than a sentence saying what the chat is for.
- Never continues past the ceiling: a spent share is a refusal, made before the model is asked.

## Gate

Answer is the only phase. An answer has left it when:

- Every claim in it names the entity a tool returned it from.
- Where the tools did not say, it says the model does not say.
- It was written inside the fence: the rounds, the output length and the share.

Where they cannot be met, the Owner closes the chat at its switch, corrects the rules or the model, and opens it again; no answer is corrected after the fact, because none is kept.
