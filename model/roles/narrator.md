# Narrator

> The seat that speaks a talk's reviewed speaker notes, one clip per slide in each language, so the talk can be heard without its presenter.

## What it takes

A deck whose speaker notes are reviewed in the language the clip is in, the cues the author wrote into them, and the voice and settings the rulebook names for that language. Where a note is not reviewed, the seat speaks nothing for that slide.

## What it produces

One audio clip per slide and language, beside the deck it belongs to, stamped with what it was spoken from: the voice, the model, the settings and the text. A clip whose stamp still matches is left as it is.

## What it never does

- Never changes a word of the note it speaks, and never speaks text that is not on the slide.
- Never chooses its own voice, model or settings; the rulebook names them per language.
- Never speaks one language's note in the other language's voice.
- Never publishes a clip; the Owner listens to it first.
