# Owner

> The seat that decides what the company is for, takes the money and the liability, and says the last word on every page.

## What it takes

The model, read before deciding: the vision, the objectives and the strategies under them, and the values a decision is weighed against. A draft or a pull request to review, on the branch and on the rendered page. The signals a strategy names as showing whether it is working.

## What it produces

Decisions, recorded where they bind: a merge, a tag, a release, a signed contract, a corrected model. The proposals and pitches the company lives on, and the advice a client pays for, since in a company of one the seat that decides is the seat that sells and delivers. The reviewed English every translation is made from. The word that ends a question an agent has parked.

## What it never does

- Never delegates a signature.
