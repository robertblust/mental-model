# Visitor

> Someone who asks about the company in their own words, on its site or through an agent of their own, and is owed what the model says and nothing else.

## What it takes

A question, in whatever words and language they have. Nothing else: the seat is filled by whoever turns up, and nobody is asked who they are.

## What it produces

A question, and where the answer sends them, a click on the entity it rests on: on the model page, or on the file at the commit it was read from.

## What it never does

- Never has to know the model's vocabulary to be answered; meeting their words is the company's work.
- Never leaves anything behind: the conversation lives in their tab and ends with it.
