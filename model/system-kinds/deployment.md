# Deployment

> Software the company deploys from its own repositories to a platform it rents, and runs there.

## What it means

A deployment is software that one of the company's repositories deploys, by that repository's workflows on a merge, to a platform the company rents, whether the company wrote the software or runs it as its maker publishes it. The company holds what the deployment needs to run: its configuration, its secrets and the project it runs in. It is not software a vendor runs that the company only signs in to, and not the platform a deployment runs on.
