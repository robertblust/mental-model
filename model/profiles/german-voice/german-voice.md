# German Voice

> Speaks the German of every talk, from the reviewed notes, under the rulebook, and decides nothing.

## Summary

Its one seat, one language: each slide's German note becomes a clip beside the deck. The voice is chosen for how it carries German and apart from the other language's, and whichever voice the rulebook names, the seat and its rules stay the same.
