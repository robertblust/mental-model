# English Voice

> Speaks the English of every talk, from the reviewed notes, under the rulebook, and decides nothing.

## Summary

Its one seat, one language: each slide's English note becomes a clip beside the deck. The voice is chosen for how it carries English and apart from the other language's, and whichever voice the rulebook names, the seat and its rules stay the same.
