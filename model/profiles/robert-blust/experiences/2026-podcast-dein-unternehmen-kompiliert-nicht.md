# Dein Unternehmen kompiliert nicht

> A podcast episode in German in which two hosts read CompanyGraph's own model and argue that a company's knowledge can be held to the standard of source code, published with a description that answers where the hosts overreach.

## Achievements

### Ways of working

- Generated the episode with Gemini Notebook from CompanyGraph's own model, the meta-model's conventions and the chat server's instructions, so the hosts argue from what the files say: that an unresolvable reference fails the check, that a reference resolves by the type its schema declares, that a decision is never rewritten but superseded, and that the chat, where the model says nothing, says exactly that.
- Read the transcript against the model, the vendored core and the chat server's prompt before publishing and kept every place where the hosts stray, to be named before anyone else names them. The one that would mislead a listener is answered in the published description: the hosts close on forking a whole company on GitHub and pressing Run, where the model describes a company and runs nothing.

### Sharing

- Published it on Sep 28, 2026 as about twenty-five minutes, the German sibling of Your company doesn't compile under the same title in German, with a description in Swiss Standard German that leans on what the English episode left out, the decision kept with the options that lost and the chat that admits what the model does not say, and hands the listener one question: could an agent read your wiki and then say what your people say.
