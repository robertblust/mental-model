# CamundaCon 2022

> Co-presented at CamundaCon 2022 in Berlin on how SV Group became a SaaS business in six months on Camunda.

## Achievements

### Context

- Co-presented *“From 'Intrapreneurs' to 'Entrepreneurs' in 6 months with user-centric innovation – Powered by Camunda”* with Markus Feller at CamundaCon 2022 in Berlin on October 5–6, 2022: how SV Group launched a new hotel brand through the crisis and turned it into a white-label SaaS business, LIKE MAGIC, on Camunda Platform 8.

### Sharing

- Took the orchestration part of the talk: each reservation a process instance, and the BPMN model the one truth, created and validated with the business and executed at runtime to monitor the guest journey.
- Showed that no client-identifying data entered Camunda's context, so monitoring the journey raised no GDPR issue.
- Set out the next step: orchestrating the journey with one custom BPMN model per tenant, executed at runtime front to back.

### Results

- Featured in Camunda's video *“Talking Process Orchestration with likeMagic's Robert Blust”*.
