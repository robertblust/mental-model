# Turn your work history into code

> A five-minute podcast episode on the career break, generated from the model's own files under a written brief and held to its sources before it went out.

## Achievements

- Generated the episode with NotebookLM from the model's own files, chosen rather than dumped: the career-break and application-tooling entries in focus, and the earlier experiences, the skills and the values as the background of the career they describe — so the two hosts had a whole career to draw on.
- Fixed in a written brief, before anything was generated, what the hosts may say: the story in outline, four threads to cover — how modeling my own career helped secure an 80% architect role, how spec-driven development and AI agents streamline an application process, company graphs to align human and agent collaboration, and how agentic workflows change an architect's daily life, told as my experience and not as a survey — an ending on an invitation in my words, and the constraints: a figure comes from a source or not at all, no employer, colleague or school is named, no salary figure is.
- Published it on Sep 6, 2026 as a five-minute episode with two hosts that tells the break as the sources tell it: one CV kept as a single source of truth in Markdown and YAML and tracked in git, 28 tailored applications generated and 25 submitted under the honesty line, an 80% IT architect role accepted with culture chosen over higher pay in finance, the model of one career as the seed of CompanyGraph, and the line “Building fast is solved. Deciding well is not.”
- Read the transcript against the sources before publishing and found one place where the episode strays: the hosts say “any gaps in employment had to be explicitly stated” where the application-tooling entry says a gap stated rather than covered, and means a gap in skills, not in employment. Recorded it as the known deviation, to be named before anyone else names it, beside the line for anyone who asks whether the hosts are real: generated from the model's own sources, reviewed by me.
