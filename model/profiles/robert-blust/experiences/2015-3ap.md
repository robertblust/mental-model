# Co-Founder & CTO

> Co-founded 3AP AG and owned technology strategy and delivery governance as CTO while the company scaled from 5 to about 70 people.

## Achievements

### Context

- Co-founded 3AP AG in November 2014 and went operative in February 2015 after leaving UBS; member of the management team.

### Leadership

- Owned technology strategy, reference architecture and delivery governance.
- Designed the company's structure of circles and roles as it grew, kept as a model on the Flatland CDO Server so capacity and gaps could be read from it rather than kept by hand.
- Set the reference architecture and technology stack every client engagement had to fit, and joined the ramp-up of each new project to negotiate with the client's security and platform organizations so the delivery fit their environment.
- Delivered flagship client platforms as lead architect and hands-on engineer across insurance, real-estate, IoT and hospitality.
- Created technical concepts, proposals and pitches.
- Advised clients including Credit Suisse, Swisscard, AXA and Mobiliar (the driver behind Aroov), diagnosing the client's current system before recommending anything.
- Led client bids with a focus on solution architecture, across both public and private sector — including public tenders under Swiss procurement law / WTO rules.
- Mentored the engineers through the reference architecture as the company grew from 5 to about 70, the near-shore team in Serbia included, by developing its reference sample in Java and Spring Boot, explaining it to each team and guiding the developers building on it.

### Architecture

- Wrote that reference architecture as an event-driven, 12-factor microservice design on Java and Spring Boot, with the service cut driven by business capability, its delivery pipeline part of the definition rather than a thing each project arranged for itself — the shape AXA Health, Flawa iQ, Conperi, Aroov and Stay KooooK were each built to, and the one that platform carried into LIKE MAGIC.
- Fixed how every service in it exposed itself: REST specified contract-first in OpenAPI, with OAuth 2.0 at the boundary.
- Put the reference architecture on MongoDB and later moved it to PostgreSQL. The first choice followed the momentum document stores had at the time rather than the shape of the data AXA Health, Flawa iQ and Conperi actually held; from Aroov onward the default was relational, and it has stayed relational through Stay KooooK and LIKE MAGIC.
- Targeted Kubernetes and no cloud in particular, which is why the same architecture ran on Google Cloud at Aroov and Stay KooooK and on AXA's own on-premise OpenShift: a client's platform was a deployment decision rather than an architectural one.

### Engineering

- Moved the reference architecture's delivery pipeline as the tooling moved, from Jenkins at Flawa iQ and Lost-Tag to CircleCI at Conperi and Ursina and then to GitHub Actions.
- Engineered the reference deployment of that architecture on Google Kubernetes Engine, so the target platform was a running cluster and not a diagram.

### Ways of working

- Established software-delivery processes, technology-stack approvals and sustainable DevOps practices.
- Ran every project in Scrum, agile delivery being what the company's name stood for: As Agile As Possible.
- Made unit and integration tests part of how a feature was finished rather than a phase after it — new behavior covered at the level that catches its regression the next time the code is touched, which is where the coverage earns its keep.

### Results

- The company scaled from 5 to about 70 people, including a near-shore team in Serbia.
- One of the flagship client platforms, the SV Group hospitality platform, became LIKE MAGIC — a spin-off from SV Group.

## Ending

Left in March 2022 after seven years. Seven years of client platforms had settled what I wanted next: to lead technology in a product company, and to stay with one product long enough to see it through. The SV Group hospitality platform spinning out as LIKE MAGIC was that chance.
