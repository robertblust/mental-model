# Co-Founder & CTO

> Co-founded 3AP AG and owned technology strategy and delivery governance as CTO while the company scaled from 5 to about 70 people.

## Achievements

- Co-founded 3AP AG in November 2014 and went operative in February 2015 after leaving UBS; member of the management team.
- The company scaled from 5 to about 70 people, including a near-shore team in Serbia.
- Owned technology strategy, reference architecture and delivery governance.
- Set the reference architecture and technology stack every client engagement had to fit, and joined the ramp-up of each new project to negotiate with the client's security and platform organizations so the delivery fit their environment.
- Wrote that reference architecture as an event-driven, 12-factor microservice design with the service cut driven by business capability, its delivery pipeline part of the definition rather than a thing each project arranged for itself — the shape AXA Health, Flawa iQ and Aroov were each built to, and the one the hospitality platform carried into LIKE MAGIC.
- Delivered flagship client platforms as lead architect and hands-on engineer across insurance, real-estate, IoT and hospitality.
- One of them, the SV Group hospitality platform, became LIKE MAGIC — a spin-off from SV Group.
- Created technical concepts, proposals and pitches.
- Advised clients including Credit Suisse, Swisscard, AXA and Mobiliar (the driver behind Aroov).
- Led client bids with a focus on solution architecture, across both public and private sector — including public tenders under Swiss procurement law / WTO rules.
- Mentored the engineers as the company grew from 5 to about 70, the near-shore team in Serbia included.
- Established software-delivery processes, technology-stack approvals and sustainable DevOps practices.
- Made unit and integration tests part of how a feature was finished rather than a phase after it — new behavior covered at the level that catches its regression the next time the code is touched, which is where the coverage earns its keep.

## Ending

Left in March 2022 after seven years. Seven years of client platforms had settled what I wanted
next: to lead technology in a product company, and to stay with one product long enough to see
it through. The SV Group hospitality platform spinning out as LIKE MAGIC was that chance.
