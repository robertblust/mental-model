# The Mental Model

> A narrated talk on the Mental Model: a company's knowledge as one structured, machine-readable base, versioned like code, that its people and its AI agents can both rely on.

## Achievements

### Context

- Gave it as a talk on blust.ch, in August 2026: a concept explained for a room that runs a company and has never seen its knowledge in one place.

### Ways of working

- Built the talk backward from one point, that the right context makes AI far more reliable, and fixed in the speaker notes what may not be claimed: the model does not remove hallucinations, it shrinks the space in which an agent has to guess and keeps it inside the guardrails of vision, strategy, process and rules, with a person overseeing and an agent's comment taken as input and never as verdict.

### Sharing

- Opened on where a company's knowledge lives, its vision, strategy, roles, processes, metrics, rules and decisions scattered across heads, wiki pages, sheets and tickets, so that neither people nor AI can rely on it, and asked what changes when all of it is a graph a machine can read.
- Showed what makes the files a graph rather than a wiki: filenames are the canonical ids, references are exact names rather than links, and a consistency check on every change surfaces a person without a role, a rule without an owner or a feature without a parent at once, so many files stay one whole without contradiction.
- Made governance concrete as data: a process gate from concept to delivery with who is accountable, what has to be true and which rule stands behind each criterion, and a rule, a metric and an architecture decision each named, justified and owned, the rejected alternative recorded beside the decision.
- Closed on why the model pays off twice: people get onboarding by role, an answer to who owns what and one place where the answer is the same for everyone, and agents get the same pages as their context layer, reached through MCP tools, and act inside the rules a person wrote rather than the ones they would invent.
- Published it as a self-contained bilingual deck with a transport bar, narration recorded from the speaker notes in each language and a PDF of each, playable from a file with no server running.
