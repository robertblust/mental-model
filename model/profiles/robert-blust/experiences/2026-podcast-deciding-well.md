# Your team isn't slow. It's undecided.

> A podcast episode in which two hosts argue out the talk that closed the career break, generated from the model's own export and a story written for it, and read against both before it went out under the talk's title.

## Achievements

### Ways of working

- Wrote the episode's story before anything was generated, as the one source beside the model's export: an opening line put straight to the listener, nine sections each naming the export file and entity it rests on, the three challenges one host puts to the other with the answer each finds in the sources, and two binding lists at its foot, what the episode may state and what it must not say: no employer, colleague or school, no salary figure, no figure the sources do not give, not that developers are obsolete, not that the proposal is proven on anyone but me, and not that the agents decided anything.
- Chose the Debate format over a narration, so that the fast half is taken at face value by one host and pushed back on hard and fair by the other, and the claim is argued rather than announced.
- Read the transcript against the story and the export before publishing: every figure spoken is in the sources and nothing on the must-not list is said. Recorded the two places where the hosts stray, to be named before anyone else names them: they say the model is read from local files, where it is Markdown in git served over MCP to any model, and they say the agents handled only the implement phase, where the model gives the plan phase to an agent seat as well and keeps every gate and every merge with the Owner.

### Sharing

- Published it on Sep 25, 2026 as four and a half minutes that open on the line "your team isn't slow, it's undecided", take the fast half from the talk's snapshot, answer the three challenges from the sources, whether the commits are padding, who did the work when an agent co-wrote nine in ten of them, and what happens if a few providers change their prices or terms, and end on the talk's proposal handed to the listener: test it with your team, and tell me where it breaks.
- Wrote the published description to carry what the hosts leave unspoken, the 272 releases, the cost against list price and the architect role from October, and to state how the episode was made: generated by Gemini Notebook from the model's own sources and the story, and checked against them before it went out.
