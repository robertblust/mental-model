# One question answered. One still open.

> The ideas page asked two questions of its two ideas. The first has an answer with a number, enough that an idea answers without its author, and the second has a test that has not been passed yet.

## Achievements

### Context

- Published the first finding the ideas page promised, answering in writing the two questions it asks of GuestGraph and CompanyGraph: how much of an idea must exist before it can be validated, and what a realistic outcome is.

### Ways of working

- Wrote the post from a reviewed specification and a plan, the English reviewed by the owner on the rendered page before any German was made, and the German made by the translator, read without the English by the editor and rendered back by the back-reader, with the owner picking among the flagged sentences.
- Checked every figure against where it stands before it went out: the hours against the talk's own figures, the cost of the subscription against the talk, and GuestGraph's objective quoted word for word.

### Results

- Answered the first question with a threshold and a cost: an idea is validated only when someone who owes it nothing can try it without its author in the room, and reaching that took an estimated 434 hours between Jun 9 and Sep 24, 2026, spent mostly on deciding rather than building.
- Answered the second without rounding it up: no revenue, because nothing is sold; no clean no yet, though both models carry the question as an objective a no settles as completely as a yes; and learning, with the first test named as adoption by a company never met rather than revenue.
- Closed on what would settle it and an ask, one company, one engagement and one hotel, and the chat on companygraph.io to ask before asking the author.
