# A career is read in order

> A reader follows over twenty-five years of work period by period: what was done in each, in what kind of engagement, and why it ended.

## Description

Each period of work is one experience, with its dates, its kind, what was achieved in it and, where it ended, an ending that says why and what it left. A talk, a film and a course are experiences too, each with its address where it has one. It stops at what happened. What a period shows about a capability is said once, as evidence under a claim, and not again here. And it is my own account: what someone else published about a period stands in that experience's references, never in its prose.
