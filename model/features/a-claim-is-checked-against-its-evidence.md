# A claim is checked against its evidence

> Someone deciding about me reads what I say I can do, and at which level, and finds under each claim the work it rests on, without asking me.

## Description

Every skill the profile claims carries a level from a ladder whose rungs are defined, and under it at least one row of evidence: one sentence naming a thing done, and the experience it comes from. An experience links the public documents it rests on — a talk's recording, a published case study, a commercial register entry — so a reader opens them for themselves. It stops at what can be shown in public: employment references, diplomas and certifications are read when a page is written, stay with me and are shown on request, so an entry resting on them carries the fact and no link. It gives no verdict either. Whether the evidence is enough for the decision at hand is the reader's to judge.
