# Sharing

> What was said, written or taught to other people.

## What it means

Talks and their content, articles and documentation written for a reader who was not there,
courses and workshops run, events organized for an audience, and communities built or led. The
bullet that says what a talk argued, or what a document explains, belongs here.

It is not the work the talk describes, which sits under its own kind, and not the decision to
publish, which is `Leadership`. A document written only for a machine — an agent file, a
prompt — is `Ways of working`.
