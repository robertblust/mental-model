# Leadership

> What was decided or owned for other people.

## What it means

Strategy, governance and the calls that bound other people: what the organization would build
and would not, how teams and roles were cut, who decided what and through which forum, the
budget held, the standards and compliance a company was held to, and the client work of advising,
bidding and negotiating.

It is not the design of a system, which is `Architecture`, and not a rule about how work is done,
which is `Ways of working`. Growing an individual — coaching, mentoring, teaching — is
`Leadership` only where the claim is about developing people; a session explaining a thing to an
audience is `Sharing`.
