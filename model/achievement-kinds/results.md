# Results

> What came of the work.

## What it means

Adoption and scale, revenue and cost outcomes, awards and recognition, the verdict of a bid, what the work became afterward, what it settled, and what it failed to reach. A lesson the period produced belongs here, and so does a plainly stated limit: what was not finished, and why.

It is not the work that produced the outcome, and it is not a figure that describes the thing rather than its effect. Where a bullet carries both the building and its result, it sits under the kind of the work when the claim is the work, and here when the claim is the outcome.
