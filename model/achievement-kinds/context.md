# Context

> What the entry is, for whom, and the part held in it.

## What it means

The bullet that says what this period was: the product, the mandate, the event, the client or
employer it was for, where it came from and what part the person played in it. A one-off entry
names itself here — the talk given, the event organized, the article published — because what it
was is the first thing a reader needs.

It is not what the work achieved. A scale figure, an award or a spin-off belongs to `Results`,
and a decision taken inside the period belongs to `Leadership` or `Architecture`. A period that
had no context worth stating skips the kind rather than restating its own title.
