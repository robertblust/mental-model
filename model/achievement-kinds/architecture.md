# Architecture

> What was designed: the structure a thing took and why.

## What it means

The shape of a system and its parts: a service cut, an interface or API, a data model or
schema, a language or metamodel, an integration path, the platform a thing targets, and the
trade-off behind any of them. A decision that fixes how something is built, before it is built,
belongs here.

It is not the building, which is `Engineering`, and not the organization's shape, which is
`Leadership`. Where a bullet names both the design and its implementation, it sits here when the
design is what the claim rests on.
