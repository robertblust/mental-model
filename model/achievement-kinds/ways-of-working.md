# Ways of working

> The method the work ran under.

## What it means

How the work was done rather than what was produced: the process followed, the specifications and briefs written before a build, the reviews and gates a change passed, the seats or roles that did the work, the conventions everyone wrote to, and the rules held to on purpose.

It is not a rule enforced by a machine, which is `Engineering`, and not a decision about what the organization does, which is `Leadership`. A method taught to other people is `Sharing` when the claim is the teaching and `Ways of working` when the claim is the method.
