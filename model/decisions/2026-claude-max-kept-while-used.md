# Claude Max 20x is kept while my use needs it

> I keep the Max 20x plan of Claude, which the agents that build the work run on, for as long as my use needs its allowance, and move to Max 5x or Pro the months it does not.

## The question

Whether the plan the work runs on is the right size. Anthropic sells Max 5x and Max 20x as five and twenty times the use of Pro, so a month whose peak used under a quarter of Max 20x would have fit Max 5x, and under a twentieth would have fit Pro. It was decided with the caps, when every paid service was first read against what it carries.

## Alternatives

| Option | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| Move to Max 5x now | During heavy development the smaller allowance runs out before the week does, and the work stops. |
| Keep Max 20x without reading its use | The plan would stay at its largest long after the building that needed it ended. |

## Why

The plan is sized to the heaviest weeks of building, and whether those weeks continue is read in Plan Fit month by month rather than assumed.

## Consequences

Each month's peak use is read by hand from the plan's usage page, since the plan offers no other reading. Several months under the smaller tier's share make a move to it a new decision that names this one.
