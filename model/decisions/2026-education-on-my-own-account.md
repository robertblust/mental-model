# The education is on my own account, and the fifth day stays mine

> I keep the 80% workload permanently and carry the education myself, its days and its fees, rather than take a full workload with days released for the school.

## The question

Whether the fifth day is bought with my own pay or granted by the employer with conditions attached. It had to be decided on September 8, 2026, when the company offered a full workload with fifteen days a year released for the school, in place of the 80% agreed on September 2.

## Alternatives

| Option | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| A full workload with fifteen days a year released for the school | Fifteen days cover the attendance and not the work: the path from a CAS through a DAS to a MAS is 60 ECTS, 1,500 to 1,800 hours over several years, around 400 a year, which is fifty days and not fifteen. |
| The employer carrying the course fees | Paid education means a training agreement with a repayment clause, which binds the choice of seminars, the pace, and whether the CAS becomes a MAS at all. |

## Why

The workload has to match the hours, and the hours are the program's, not the employer's to grant. And the education is worth more unbound: which seminars, at what pace, and whether it goes all the way to a master's are calls I want to keep, which a repayment clause would take. Money for independence, knowingly.

## Consequences

A fifth of the pay and the course fees carried privately for as long as the education runs. The path, a CAS first, then a DAS, then a MAS, runs beside the role and on my own clock. What has to stay true is that the fifth day stays free of the role's work; the day it fills with it, the trade was made for nothing.
