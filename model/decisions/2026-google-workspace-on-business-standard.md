# Google Workspace stays on Business Standard for two seats

> I keep Google Workspace on Business Standard with two seats, because Gemini and Gemini Notebook, which the work uses, come with that tier.

## The question

Whether Workspace needs Business Standard, with its pooled storage and its advanced AI models, or would be carried by Business Starter, and whether both seats are used. It was decided with the caps, before the trial discount ends in October 2026 and the full price starts.

## Alternatives

| Option | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| Business Starter | It does not carry the Gemini features the work uses. |
| One seat | The second seat is the one Gemini and Gemini Notebook are used from. |

## Why

The tier is chosen for its AI features and not for its storage, so Plan Fit reads it by whether the month used what only this tier provides, and the storage share alone would argue for Starter wrongly.

## Consequences

A month that used no Gemini feature is a month the smaller tier would have carried, and several such months make a move to Starter a new decision.
