# The Anthropic API is capped at USD 20 a month

> The Anthropic API, which the three chats answer through, may cost at most USD 20 in a calendar month, and the workspace's monthly limit is set to that amount so the provider stops it there.

## The question

What the API that answers every chat question may cost. It had to be decided on the day the workspace's monthly limit was reached and all three chats stopped answering, because the limit then in place had been set with no amount decided behind it.

## Alternatives

| Option | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| No limit, and read the bill | A question asked in a loop would cost whatever it cost, and I would learn of it a month later. |
| A limit set high enough never to be reached | A cap that cannot bite decides nothing, and the amount would say what I fear rather than what I mean to spend. |

## Why

USD 20 a month is what the chats cost in the priced account of the Deciding well talk, which is the chats answering visitors and not me building them. A limit the provider enforces is the only kind that stops spending rather than reporting it.

## Consequences

When the month's spend reaches the cap, every chat stops answering until the month turns, so the chat meter's daily share has to sit below a thirtieth of the cap, where it refuses a visitor politely before the workspace refuses everyone. It stays right while the chats cost about what they cost now; a chat that answers more questions, or answers them at more length, raises it by a new decision.
