# ChatGPT Plus is kept only in months it is used

> I keep ChatGPT Plus for as long as a month uses something only the plan provides, and end it when months pass without that.

## The question

Whether a subscription outside the work, used privately and not in anything the model describes, earns its place. Its only smaller tier is free and it shows no meter, so its fit is a question of whether it was used at all. It was decided with the caps, when every paid service was first read against what it carries.

## Alternatives

| Option | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| End it now | Whether it is used is what Plan Fit reads, and ending it before the first reading would decide without one. |
| Leave it out as private | A subscription paid every month is a service I pay for, whatever it is used for. |

## Why

Plus has no smaller paid tier, so the only fit in question is the plan or none, and that is read from use and not from price.

## Consequences

Plan Fit reads it by whether the month used what only Plus provides. Months without that make ending it a new decision; it is billed through Google Play and ended there.
