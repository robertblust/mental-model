# My Claude sessions do not train Anthropic's models

> The Claude account I work with, the one the agents that build the work run on, has “Help improve our AI models” switched off, so Anthropic does not use its chats and coding sessions to train and improve its models: not the sessions in which this model, my sites and my products are built, and not the work I do for others.

## The question

Anthropic offers the account a setting, “Help improve our AI models”, that allows it to use the account's chats and coding sessions to train and improve its models. One account carries all my work with Claude, my own and the work I do for others, so the setting decides for both at once. It had to be decided before work that is not only mine went through the account.

## Alternatives

| Option | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| Leave the setting on | Work I do for others would become material for training a model others use, and that is a call about their information which is not mine to make. |
| Leave it on and use a separate account for confidential work | The line between the two accounts would be drawn by hand in every session, and one session started in the wrong account puts confidential work where it trains a model; the allowance the plan is sized for would also be split in two. |

## Why

One switch covers every session, so nothing depends on my judging, session by session, what a piece of work may be used for. What I give up is a contribution to Anthropic's models that my work does not need; what it protects is work that is partly not mine to give.

## Consequences

The setting stays off for as long as the account carries work that is not mine to give, which gives up whatever my sessions would have added to Anthropic's models, and I read it again whenever Anthropic changes its consumer terms or the setting. What the call does not change is that Anthropic still receives and processes every request the account sends, and keeps what it keeps under its own terms and for its own purposes, as the Anthropic page says. Whether a piece of work I do for others may be given to Claude at all is not mine to decide here. The call stays right for as long as this setting is what decides whether the account's sessions are used to train a model.
