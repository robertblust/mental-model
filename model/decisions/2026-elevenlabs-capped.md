# ElevenLabs is capped at USD 20 a month in top-ups

> ElevenLabs, which gives the talks their recorded voice, is paid by credits bought as they run out, and in a calendar month I buy at most USD 20 of them.

## The question

What the recorded voice may cost, on a service with no plan to cap it: credits are topped up, and every clip re-recorded after an edit spends them. It was decided with the other caps, when what each paid service may cost was written down for the first time.

## Alternatives

| Option | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| Top up whenever credits run out | A month of re-recorded talks would cost what it cost, with nothing to say it was too much. |
| A monthly plan in place of top-ups | A plan is paid in the months that record nothing. |

## Why

Recording follows writing, so what it costs follows what I publish, and a month that needs more than USD 20 is a month to ask whether the recordings are worth it before paying for them.

## Consequences

The cap is kept by me, not by the provider: a top-up is a purchase I make, and the month's total is read from the provider's billing. A month that needs more is a new decision before the top-up, not a finding after it.
