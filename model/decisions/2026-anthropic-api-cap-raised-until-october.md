# The Anthropic API cap is raised to USD 50 until October 31, 2026

> Until October 31, 2026 the Anthropic API may cost up to USD 50 a month, and the workspace limit is set there; from November it returns to USD 20.

## The question

Whether the cap holds during a period of heavy development on the chats, in which each change is measured against the API before it ships. It had to be decided the day the workspace limit stopped the chats, with that development still under way.

## Alternatives

| Option | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| Keep USD 20 through the development | The measurements that decide a chat change would stop the live chats each time they ran against the same workspace. |
| Raise the cap with no end date | An exception without an end is the new cap under another name, and nothing would bring it back. |

## Why

The extra spend comes from building, not from visitors, and it ends when the building does; an end date makes that claim checkable, because a November reading over USD 20 would break the cap and not the exception.

## Consequences

Cap Use reads over the whole in October against the USD 20 cap, and this decision is what says so on purpose. On November 1, 2026 the workspace limit goes back to USD 20; extending past that date is a new decision that names this one in `supersedes`.
