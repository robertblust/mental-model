# Community

> Work done in public — a talk, a seat, a working group, a published case.

## What it means

A period whose output is addressed to a public rather than to one organization: a conference
talk, a board or committee seat, a user group, a case study published under someone else's
name, a tool put out in the open. Who paid for the time does not decide the kind; an employer
can host a talk or publish an article and the period is still this kind, because the audience
is. `organization` names the host where there is one — the conference, the group, the
publisher, the employer when it published the work itself — and is absent when nobody hosted
it.

A talk writes `end` equal to `start`; a board seat held for years does not. Both are this kind:
what makes it community work is the audience, not the duration.
