# Project

> A delivery worth naming on its own, inside a role or beside one.

## What it means

A period defined by what it produced rather than by whom it was spent with: a platform, a
migration, a launch. Client work and internal work alike. `organization` names the client, or
whoever the work was done for when that is not the employer. Where the client cannot be named,
it names the employer the work was done through, and the body says the client is unnamed.

It ends when the delivery does. A project with no end and no deliverable is a role described by
its current work.
