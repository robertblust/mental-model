# Role

> A position held in an organization — the employment history.

## What it means

A period spent working for an organization under an arrangement that outlasts any one piece of
work: employment, a long contract, a partnership. `organization` names the employer.

It is not a delivery. What was built during a role is a project of its own, and a role whose
achievements describe one system is usually two periods written as one. The test is whether the
period would have continued had the work changed: if it would, it is a role.
