# Independent

> A self-directed period with no employer and no client.

## What it means

A period worked for oneself: what to build and whether to build it were the same person's
decision, and nobody commissioned the result. `organisation` is absent, because there is none —
which is the difference a reader would otherwise have to infer from an empty field.

It is not a project: nobody asked for the delivery, and the period is not bounded by it. It is
not a role: there is no organization to hold a position in. Those two readings are what this
kind exists to avoid having to choose between — a career break spent building products is
neither, and saying so is cheaper than pretending it is one of them.
