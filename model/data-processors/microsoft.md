# Microsoft

> Signs a reader in to id.blust.ch with their Microsoft account, a personal one or one their organization gives them, when the reader chooses Sign in with Microsoft, and tells id.blust.ch the account's id, its name and the email address the account names and, for a sign-in not yet linked to an account there, whether the account is a personal one or which domains the reader's organization has verified with Microsoft, an answer id.blust.ch asks for with that sign-in's access token, uses only to decide whether to link the account or to take a new account's address as verified, and keeps nowhere; it may process the sign-in in any country it chooses.

## Own purposes

- Keeps, for a personal Microsoft account, that the reader let id.blust.ch read their profile, as an app the reader can review and remove in their Microsoft account's privacy settings, under Microsoft's privacy statement, for which Microsoft Corporation is the controller, and Microsoft Ireland Operations Limited too for readers in the European Economic Area.
- Records, for an account the reader's organization gives them, the sign-in in that organization's directory, which the organization controls; Microsoft's privacy statement says of such an account that "your organization controls your account and can access and process your data".
