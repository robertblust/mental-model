# Cost

> Questions about what the work costs and how that cost is kept in check: what running it costs, and how Robert keeps cloud spend under control.

## What it means

A question whose subject is money going out: what a service may cost, how its cap or plan is decided and read, and what building the work cost. It is this kind even when it asks about Robert's practice, because what it asks is what things cost.

A question about the chat or the model that does not ask what it costs is Model and chat.
