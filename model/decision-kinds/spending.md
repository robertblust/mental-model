# Spending

> A call about what I pay for a service: which plan, and how much a month it may cost.

## What it means

A decision whose subject is money going out to a provider: a cap set on a metered service, an exception that raises it for a while, a subscription taken, kept, moved to another tier or ended. It is this kind even when the service runs a product, because what was decided is what it may cost.

What a product is and how it is built is Portfolio, even when the call makes it cost more; the cap that follows is Spending. A call about which role to take is Career.
