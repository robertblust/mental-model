# Architecture

> A call about the machinery my repositories share: the tooling, the checks, and how the work people and agents do reaches the history.

## What it means

A decision whose subject is how the work is carried out across my repositories rather than what the work is: a check every repository runs, a rule a hook enforces, whose name a commit carries. It is this kind even when it changes what a seat does, because what was decided is the machinery the seats work inside.

Which product I build and in what form is Portfolio, and what a service may cost is Spending. How one product's own components are built is decided in its own repositories and recorded in its own model.
