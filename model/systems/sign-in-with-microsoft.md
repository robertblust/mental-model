# Sign in with Microsoft

> Signs a reader in with their Microsoft account, a personal one or one their organization gives them, when the reader chooses it on the login of id.blust.ch, and returns to id.blust.ch the account's id, its name and the email address the account names; id.blust.ch signs the reader in only where that account is already linked to the account the owner made for them, a link the reader makes when they accept their invitation or the owner makes at their request, and never by the address; it opens no account of its own.
